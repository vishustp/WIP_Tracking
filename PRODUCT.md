# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Seamless steel pipe manufacturing plant managers, production supervisors, shift quality inspectors, and machine operators across hot rolling, heat treatment, cold drawing, band saw cutting, and NDT/VDI inspection stations.

## Product Purpose

Real-time manufacturing execution, Work-In-Progress (WIP) ledger tracking, multi-length band saw cutting optimization, quality salvage dispositions, and production reporting for high-precision seamless steel pipes.

## Positioning

Industrial-grade seamless pipe production management system with ASTM/ASME/BS/EN metallurgical compliance, route-stage WIP balancing, multi-length cut yield recovery, and gated quality workflows.

## Operating Context

Shop-floor workstations, plant tablet devices, production office desktops with high-frequency logging of pipe metrics (OD, WT, Length, PCS, MTTR, MT) and scrap generation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stage-wise production logging (Piercing, Cold Draw, Heat Treatment, Band Saw Cutting, VDI/QC Inspection, Hydro Testing, Dispatch).
- Real-time mother pipe balance tracking and scrap loss calculation in Metric Tonnes (MT).
- Work Order lifecycle tracking with heat/lot numbers and route codes (CDS, HFS, ALLOY_CDS, ALLOY_HFS).
- Matrix-based Size & Grade WIP reporting with accessible high-contrast industrial tables.

## Brand Commitments

- Clean, high-density industrial UI with high legibility and unambiguous operational state indicators.
- Precision numerical typography using monospaced font styles for gauges, metrics, and weights.
- Color semantics: Emerald for Prime/Good, Indigo for Processed, Amber for Scrap/Warning, Rose for Defects/Alerts.

## Product Principles

1. **Zero Material Ambiguity**: Every meter and tonne of mother pipe must balance (Prime + Usable Offcut + Scrap = Mother Input).
2. **Speed & Ergonomics**: Fast data entry for busy operators with 1-click presets and keyboard navigation.
3. **Auditable Traceability**: Every production entry preserves heat lot numbers, operator remarks, and timestamps.
