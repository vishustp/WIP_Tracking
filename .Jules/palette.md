## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** This app often uses Lucide icons inside `<button>` tags without providing an `aria-label` or `title` for screen readers and tooltips (e.g., in DataReport and WorkOrderTrackingClient).
**Action:** When working on UI components, routinely check icon-only buttons for missing accessibility attributes and proactively add them.
