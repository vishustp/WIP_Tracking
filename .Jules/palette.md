## 2025-02-27 - [A11y Pattern: Icon-only Action Buttons]
**Learning:** Across our admin datatables (like Spec Master and likely others), row-level action buttons (Edit, Delete, Toggle Active) frequently rely exclusively on `title` attributes for tooltips, omitting `aria-label`s. This makes them invisible or confusing to screen readers because `title` is insufficient on its own for accessibility. When adding `aria-label`s, it's crucial to incorporate dynamic row data (e.g., the record's name) rather than generic labels like "Edit" so screen reader users know exactly *which* item they are modifying without needing surrounding context.
**Action:** When reviewing or creating data tables with row-level actions, always ensure a descriptive `aria-label` is present on icon-only buttons, and that it dynamically references the specific record being acted upon.
